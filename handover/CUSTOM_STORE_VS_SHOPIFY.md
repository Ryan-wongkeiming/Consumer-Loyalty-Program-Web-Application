# CareHub — Custom Store vs. Shopify

**One-page overview for the client**
**Date:** 2026-09-18

---

## The one-line answer

> **CareHub is a custom-built store that already does what you need.**
> **Shopify would be a replacement, not an upgrade.**

---

## Why this question comes up

Clients sometimes ask: *"Why didn't you build this on Shopify?"* It is a fair question —
Shopify is a famous e-commerce platform. The answer is **not** "Shopify is bad."
The answer is: *"We built a custom store because your market and your business model
need things Shopify does not do well."*

---

## The big picture

| | **Option A — Custom store (CareHub)** | **Option B — Shopify** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Your own website + your own database, built for you | A rented, complete e-commerce system |
| Monthly cost | ~$0–25 (Supabase + hosting) | $29–299/month + transaction fees + apps |
| Build time from today | **Already live** — done | **Weeks of rebuild** |
| Who owns the data | **You** (your Supabase database) | Shopify (you rent access) |
| Who owns the design | **You, 100%** | Limited to Shopify templates |
| Who maintains it | Your team | Shopify maintains the platform |

---

## Feature comparison — what really matters

| Feature you already have | Custom store (CareHub) | Shopify |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscriptions (Đăng ký & Tiết kiệm)** — 15% (infant formula 10%), free shipping | ✅ **Native, exactly as designed** | ⚠️ Needs paid apps; COD + "confirm each cycle" is a poor fit |
| **COD-first payment** (pay on delivery, no auto-charge) | ✅ Built-in | ⚠️ Built for card payments first; COD is clunky |
| **Confirmation before every delivery** via Zalo/WhatsApp/SMS/email | ✅ Built-in | ⚠️ Manual workaround |
| **Multi-brand store** (4 brands, 1 shop) | ✅ Built-in (brand filter) | ⚠️ One store = one brand; multi-brand needs workarounds |
| **Buy More Save More** (3 = −20%, 5 = −35%) | ✅ Built-in | ⚠️ Basic discounts only; complex bundles need apps |
| **Loyalty points + free samples** | ✅ Built-in | ⚠️ Each needs a third-party app (paid, monthly) |
| **Checkout** (COD / manual bank transfer) | ✅ Yours, matches Vietnamese habits | ⚠️ Card-first checkout |
| **Promo-code OCR camera** | ✅ Built-in | ⚠️ Not available; custom app needed |

---

## What Shopify genuinely does better

| Shopify strength | What it means |
|---|---|
| Battle-tested online checkout & card payments | Higher conversion **if** your customers pay by card |
| Huge app store (reviews, shipping, analytics) | Install instead of build |
| Real inventory & fulfillment tools | Good for large warehouses |
| Professional themes without a developer | Fast to look professional |
| Recognized brand name | Some clients explicitly want "runs on Shopify" |

**Honest note:** if your customers pay mostly by credit card and you want to manage
everything through a standard admin panel, *those two reasons alone* can justify
Shopify. For the Vietnamese market with COD + Zalo-confirmed subscriptions,
they usually do not.

---

## The two cases

| If the client says… | Direction |
|---|---|
| "We want a working store with bundles, subscriptions, loyalty — like the demo" | ✅ **Custom store** — already built, live |
| "We specifically want Shopify" | ⚠️ **Rebuild** — new project, weeks of work, higher cost, loses features |
| "We need card payments" | ⚠️ Evaluate — the custom store can add a payment gateway (Stripe, etc.) **without** switching |
| "We want to manage products easily" | ✅ Either — CareHub can get a simple admin screen; Shopify wins only if they want to *use* Shopify |
| "We want to sell on HAPPI/Little Oak's stores too" | 🔀 That is a **separate channel**, not a reason to rebuild yours |

---

## Our recommendation

**Stay on the custom store — it is a strength, not a compromise.**

- It was built for **this market**: COD-first, Zalo-confirmed subscriptions,
  multi-brand, bundles, loyalty — none of which Shopify does out of the box.
- It is **already live** and working.
- It costs **~$0–25/month** instead of $29–299/month + fees.
- You own the data and the design.

**If a client insists on Shopify**, the honest response is:
*"That is a rebuild. Here is what it costs and what we would lose."*
Then it is their informed decision — and we can build it if they pay for it.
